# The same screens as `baseline.txt`, in the **light** appearance (ADR-0036).
#
# It switches the theme through the real control on Settings rather than through a test hook, which
# is deliberate: the appearance is a stored, device-local preference, so a capture that forced it
# some other way would photograph a state the application cannot actually be put into. It also means
# this storyboard fails loudly if `theme_control` stops working, where an env override would keep
# producing light pictures of a broken control.
#
# **Settings is visited first, and that is the whole reason the order differs from `baseline.txt`.**
# Every screen after the switch is drawn light; anything captured before it would be whatever the
# host's System preference resolves to, which is not reproducible between machines.
#
# `%CX%` is the centre of the output; `%LX+n%` is `n` px inside the page frame's left edge (#131).
#
# The first click is spent giving the window keyboard focus and never reaches a widget, so it is
# aimed at empty space.
mousemove %CX% 600 click 1
sleep 1
# Settings. The nav row sits above the frame's content but aligns to the same left edge.
mousemove %LX+140% 11 click 1
sleep 1.2
# **Appearance: Light, reached by keyboard rather than by a coordinate.** Since #152 the control closes
# the *This device* group, below the rate, scheduler and sync prose — and that prose wraps at 560 and
# not at 1280, so no single y reaches it at both widths. #143 first met that miss as seven perfectly
# valid *dark* captures with nothing failing, and answered it by moving the control to the top of the
# page; #152 put the page in its own order, so the storyboard changes instead.
#
# **Focus order is the order the page is built in, and no width moves it.** From an unfocused window,
# Tab visits the nav row (Review, Notes, Settings), the new-card-rate field, *Optimise*, *Set up sync*,
# then `System` and `Light` — eight Tabs, and Space presses what has focus. It holds against every
# fixture, because each of those controls is drawn whatever the collection holds; only a **file set**
# adds rows before Appearance, and no light storyboard names one. The `--delay` puts each key in a
# frame of its own, since egui moves focus once per frame.
key --delay 150 Tab Tab Tab Tab Tab Tab Tab Tab space
sleep 1.5
# Click the empty end of the nav band, which clears the focus ring off `Light` before the shot.
mousemove %CX% 11 click 1
sleep 1
shot 06-settings-top
# Settings is longer than any window this pass uses. Eight notches show where *This device* ends and
# the Development section begins.
mousemove %CX% 400 click 5 click 5 click 5 click 5 click 5 click 5 click 5 click 5
sleep 1.2
shot 07-settings-scrolled
# Then to the end of the page, where the specimens are since #152 put them under Development. Worth
# capturing in light specifically: the rendering specimen is the one place the app draws every family
# at once, and a light ground is where a wrong face is most visible. Forty notches is past the end at
# both widths, and the scroll area stops there, so no count has to be exact.
mousemove %CX% 400 click --repeat 40 --delay 30 5
sleep 1.2
shot 08-settings-end
# Wind the shared scroll area back up before leaving, or the next destination opens mid-page.
mousemove %CX% 400 click --repeat 60 --delay 30 4
sleep 1
# Review, in light. This is the screen ADR-0033 §3 is about — the card must still outweigh the
# grades, and it is the pair the ink construction was chosen to preserve.
mousemove %LX+27% 11 click 1
sleep 1.2
shot 01-review-start
# The count picker sits at the start of its line: `5` then `All 5`. `+39` reaches `All 5`.
mousemove %LX+39% 129 click 1
sleep 1.5
shot 02-review-question
# Reveal is tap-the-card (review.rs: "the prompt is one wide button").
mousemove %CX% 148 click 1
sleep 1.5
shot 03-review-revealed
mousemove %LX+86% 11 click 1
sleep 1.2
shot 04-notes-list
# The editor is visited last, for `baseline.txt`'s reason: on a window wide enough for two columns it
# takes a wider frame and the nav row follows it, so any `%LX+n%` nav click after this point would
# land on empty page.
mousemove %CX% 400 click 4 click 4 click 4 click 4
sleep 1
mousemove %LX+19% 286 click 1
sleep 1.5
shot 05-notes-editor
