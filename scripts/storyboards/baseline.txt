# The screens reachable from a first launch, in one pass. Runs unchanged at any width.
#
# `%CX%` is the centre of the output. Use it for anything full-width — which is almost everything the
# app draws.
#
# `%LX+n%` is `n` px inside the **page frame's** left edge, and it is what a control packed against
# the start of a line now needs. A literal x was right only while content ran to the window edge; the
# frame centres the column, so the same button is at 320+n at 1280 and 28+n at 560 (#131).
#
# **The editor is visited last, and that is load-bearing rather than tidy.** On a window wide enough
# for two columns the editor takes a wider frame, and the nav row follows it — so the nav is at a
# different x while the editor is open, and any `%LX+n%` nav click *after* this point would land on
# empty page. Leaving by *Done* is no better: that button is compact at 1280 and full-width at 560,
# so no single coordinate reaches it at both. Ending here costs nothing and needs no coordinate that
# is right at only one width.
#
# The first click is spent giving the window keyboard focus and never reaches a widget, so it is
# aimed at empty space.
mousemove %CX% 600 click 1
sleep 1
shot 01-review-start
# The count picker sits at the start of its line: `5` then `All 5`.
mousemove %LX+39% 93 click 1
sleep 1.5
shot 02-review-question
# Reveal is tap-the-card (review.rs: "the prompt is one wide button").
mousemove %CX% 131 click 1
sleep 1.5
shot 03-review-revealed
mousemove %LX+86% 11 click 1
sleep 1.2
shot 04-notes-list
mousemove %LX+140% 11 click 1
sleep 1.2
shot 06-settings-top
# Settings is longer than any window this pass uses, so the font specimen needs scrolling to.
mousemove %CX% 400 click 5 click 5 click 5 click 5 click 5 click 5 click 5 click 5
sleep 1.2
shot 07-settings-scrolled
# Back to Notes. The scroll area is shared between destinations and keeps its offset, so wind it
# back up before aiming at a row — otherwise the click lands wherever Settings left the page.
mousemove %LX+86% 11 click 1
sleep 1.2
mousemove %CX% 400 click 4 click 4 click 4 click 4 click 4 click 4 click 4 click 4
sleep 1
# Open the first note — `chien`, which has a card — rather than *Create note*. A fresh draft has no
# cards, so the editor's card pane would read "No cards yet" and the two-column layout this pass
# exists to photograph would have nothing in half of it.
mousemove %LX+19% 221 click 1
sleep 1.5
shot 05-notes-editor
