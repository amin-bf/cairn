# **Motion and elevation** (ADR-0037) — the reveal and the one surface that floats, in **dark**.
#
# Runs on the `cloze` fixture rather than the seed, and that is the whole point of the storyboard.
# A vocabulary card grows by a *word* at the reveal; a cloze card grows by a whole *face*, because
# its two faces are the same sentence differing by one masked word. Only the second shape reaches
# ADR-0033 §4's step-down, and until ADR-0037 §5 the step-down fired **at the reveal** — the prompt
# drawn at display before the tap and at heading after it. No capture in this repository could ever
# have shown that: every one is of six French words, and no card in that seed is long enough.
#
# Coordinates, frame-relative and identical at 1280x800 and 560x860:
#   nav y=11: Review %LX+27%, Notes %LX+86%, Settings %LX+140%
#   the entrance's one primary: %CX% y=129 — full width, so the centre reaches it at either width
#   the card: %CX% y=254 — the whole face is the reveal target (ADR-0006 §3)
#   the grades: %CX% %BY-183% reaches *Good*
#   the deck filter's combo box: %LX+50% y=109; the new-deck field %LX+300% y=137, its button %LX+36%
#   — all three moved up in #162, which took *Create note* off the top of the page
#
# **The grade row is the one click here that a literal y cannot reach**, and this storyboard was
# written wrong before it was written right. ADR-0035 §1 anchors the last control cluster to a line
# above the **bottom of the page**, so its y is a function of the window height: *Good* is at 677 in
# an 860-tall window and 617 in an 800-tall one. A literal 677 lands on it at 560x860 and on empty
# page at 1280x800 — and the run still produces five perfectly valid images, of the previous card,
# under the next card's names. That is the silent miss `%CX%` and `%LX+n%` were introduced to kill,
# arriving from the third axis, which is what `%BY-n%` is for. 183 is the same at both because the
# reach line is the same at both.
#
# The first click is spent giving the window keyboard focus and never reaches a widget, so it is
# aimed at empty space.
fixture cloze
mousemove %CX% 600 click 1
sleep 1

# --- the reveal, on a card that fits ---------------------------------------------------------------
mousemove %CX% 129 click 1
sleep 1.5
# **Shut.** The prompt sits on the card's own centre line — no badge, no hairline, no empty half.
# This is the frame the wipe opens from, and it is pixel-identical to what the card drew before
# ADR-0037: if it ever drifts one badge row low again, that is the closed lead failing to pay for
# the row it draws (ADR-0037's consequences), and it is visible here and nowhere else.
shot 01-reveal-shut
mousemove %CX% 254 click 1
sleep 1.5
# **Open.** Both faces, the hairline between them, the badge arrived. The card's own rect is
# unchanged between these two frames; what moved is the boundary inside it.
shot 02-reveal-open

# --- the reveal, on a card that steps down ---------------------------------------------------------
# Grade *Good* to reach the second card, whose faces overflow the budget at 560. **This pair is the
# evidence for ADR-0037 §5**: the prompt must be at the same tier and the same wrap in both shots.
mousemove %CX% %BY-183% click 1
sleep 1.5
shot 03-stepdown-shut
mousemove %CX% 254 click 1
sleep 1.5
shot 04-stepdown-open

# --- the overlay ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Two decks first, so the popup has three rows: a one-row popup is too small a thing to judge a
# shadow against, and no fixture ships a deck.
mousemove %LX+86% 11 click 1
sleep 1.5
mousemove %LX+300% 137 click 1
sleep 0.5
sh xdotool type --delay 40 'Cuisine'
sleep 0.5
mousemove %LX+36% 137 click 1
sleep 1
mousemove %LX+300% 137 click 1
sleep 0.5
sh xdotool type --delay 40 'Voyage'
sleep 0.5
mousemove %LX+36% 137 click 1
sleep 1
# **The only thing in the application that floats** (ADR-0037 §1). Before this it was drawn in
# *exactly* the page colour inside stock egui's unassigned grey hairline with no shadow, in every
# capture this repository holds. It should now read as a slab sitting on top of the page.
mousemove %LX+50% 109 click 1
sleep 1
shot 05-overlay
