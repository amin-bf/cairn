# The card pane's other two entry shapes, and the warning above the fields.
#
# ADR-0018 gives the pane three shapes — a card, a **dormant line** (§2), and a statement for a note
# that generates none (§6) — and ADR-0025 §4 puts the destructive-edit warning above the form's
# fields. Two of the three and the warning had never been drawn by anything, because reaching
# dormancy needs a slot with kept reviews that content stopped generating and no fixture asked for
# one. `dormant` is that fixture.
#
# Three notes, one per naming case (ADR-0018 §3): the pruned cloze note keeps a live card beside a
# `blank 2` line, the emptied vocab note has nothing live and two lines naming field **roles**, and
# the returned note carries `card 7` — the slot no shipped kind declares.
#
# **Each note is reached through Search rather than by counting rows.** #162 made a row a 44px
# full-width band and moved *Create note* to the foot of the list, so every literal row y in this
# file was wrong the moment that landed — and the ninth note fell below the fold at 800 besides. A
# filter names the note; an offset counts the furniture.
#
# **The row click aims past the preview text, and that is deliberate.** A row's preview is drawn as an
# egui label, labels are *selectable* by default, and a selectable label senses click and drag — so a
# press landing on the words was swallowed and the row simply did not open, while the same row opened
# from the bare band beside them. That made it look like particular *notes* were dead rather than
# particular *pixels*: the long cloze preview reaches under the middle of its band and `le hêtre` two
# rows down does not. Fixed in #177; aiming past the text is correct either way and costs nothing.
