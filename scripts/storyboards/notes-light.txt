# The same screens as `notes.txt`, in the **light** appearance (ADR-0036).
#
# It switches the theme through the real control on Settings rather than through a test hook, for
# `light.txt`'s reason: the appearance is a stored, device-local preference, so a capture that forced
# it some other way would photograph a state the application cannot be put into.
#
# **Settings is visited first**, and that is why the order differs from `notes.txt`. Anything
# captured before the switch is whatever the host's System preference resolves to, which is not
# reproducible between machines.
#
# Two of #150's three questions are about **material** rather than arrangement — whether a card and
# a text field may share a fill, and what the Cards pane is — and #143 found that ADR-0033 §3's rule
# passes in both themes while its *values* collapse in one of them. So the light set is evidence
# here rather than a completeness exercise.

# control sits directly under a one-word heading that cannot wrap (#143).

mousemove %LX+84% 93 click 1
sleep 1.5
