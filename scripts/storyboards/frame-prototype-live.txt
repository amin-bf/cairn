# A smoke test for the prototype's interactive mode, driven by clicking the nav.
#
# Not a judging set — the stills in `frame-prototype.txt` are that. This exists because the README
# tells a reader `PROTO_SCREEN=live` gives them a click-through, and a claim about a mode nobody has
# run is the kind of thing #122 found fails silently.
#
# **The move and the press are separate lines with a settle between them.** Sent as one
# `mousemove x y click 1`, egui can see the press before it has taken the motion, and resolves the
# click against the pointer's *previous* position — which lands on nothing and looks exactly like a
# dead control. The app's own storyboards get away with the combined form because they follow every
# click with `sleep 1`; this one says it out loud.
#
# The nav pills sit at the frame's left edge, which moves with the frame, so these coordinates are
# written for F1 at 1280 and are not width-portable. Acceptable for a smoke test, not for a capture
# set.

shot L1-review

mousemove 430 24
sleep 0.5
click 1
sleep 1
shot L2-notes

mousemove 500 24
sleep 0.5
click 1
sleep 1
shot L3-settings

mousemove 358 24
sleep 0.5
click 1
sleep 1
shot L4-back-to-review
