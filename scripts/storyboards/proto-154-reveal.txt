# PROTOTYPE #154 — the reveal, five candidates at three points in the transition.
#
# **A still cannot photograph a jump**, which is why the reveal is judged as a running sitting and
# not from these. What these are for is the write-up: the middle frame of a transition is the thing
# the argument is about, and a settled capture — four seconds after the tap — can never reach it. So
# the prototype can *hold* `t` at a fraction, and this walks the grid.
#
# Fifteen shots: hold 0%, 50% and 100% × candidates A, B, C, D and E. At 0% the card is drawn as it
# looks *before* the tap, with `revealed` already true — which is how the reserved candidates' empty
# lower half becomes photographable at all.
#
# **Every coordinate here is frame-relative and identical at 1280x800 and 560x860**, which was
# measured rather than assumed: the switcher sits directly under the one-word heading, above
# Appearance's sentence, and that sentence is the thing that wraps at 560 and moves everything under
# it by 17px.
#
# The switcher's rows, all at `%LX+80%` except the hold row:
#   A 101   B 119   C 137   D 156   E 174
#   hold:  running %LX+29%   0% %LX+81%   25% %LX+120%   50% %LX+164%   75% %LX+207%   100% %LX+254%
#   at y=283
# The nav row is y=11: Review %LX+27%, Notes %LX+86%, Settings %LX+140%.
#
# The first click is spent giving the window keyboard focus and never reaches a widget, so it is
# aimed at empty space.
mousemove %CX% 600 click 1
sleep 1
