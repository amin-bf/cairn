# **The Settings half of the fixture bench, driven** — the half that reaches the handset.
#
# The desktop harness never needs this block: it installs a fixture from outside, with
# `cairn-fixture`, before the app starts. Android has no such route — `getFilesDir()` is not writable
# from outside the app, and #141 found that an uninstall is not a first launch there either — so a
# thumb tapping this row is the only way in on a phone, and #126 judges every destination and
# sub-state that way.
#
# **So this storyboard exists to keep the handset from being where the block is first found broken.**
#
# **Run it at 1280×800 and nowhere else.** It is a verification rather than a design capture, and
# unlike every other storyboard here it is *not* width-independent: the bench sits below three
# paragraphs of prose that wrap at one width and not the other, so both the scroll count and the y
# are measured at that size — the exact shape of the miss #143 hit. That is tolerated here because
# both shots are **self-evidencing**: a tap that missed leaves `10-installed-from-settings` showing
# Settings rather than the caught-up floor, and leaves the bench with nothing to say in
# `11-bench-verdict`. Measured at 560×860, it misses — the row is at y=625 there.
mousemove %CX% 600 click 1
sleep 1
# Settings, then down to the specimens.
mousemove %LX+140% 11 click 1
sleep 1.2
mousemove %CX% 400 click 5 click 5 click 5 click 5 click 5 click 5
sleep 1.2
# `Nothing due`, the first of the seven — the leftmost, so its x is the one in the row that survives
# the row wrapping. That wrapping is no longer hypothetical: since #161 the block draws two lines,
# and every button but this one has moved at least once.
mousemove %LX+60% 557 click 1
sleep 1.5
# Installing a fixture lands you on Review, because looking at the state is the whole purpose. The
# caught-up floor here is the proof the tap did what it says.
shot 10-installed-from-settings
# The bench's own verdict, kept on Settings for the return trip: what the collection actually
# reached, or why it did not. A handset has no console to print it to.
mousemove %LX+140% 11 click 1
sleep 1.2
mousemove %CX% 400 click 5 click 5 click 5 click 5 click 5 click 5
sleep 1.2
shot 11-bench-verdict
