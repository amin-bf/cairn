Persian in the editor

The Note Editor's last design question. By chrome I mean the app's own words and controls — the heading, Kind, Deck, the Front / Back labels, Done — as opposed to what a note holds. The question: when a note is Persian, does any of the chrome move with it? Every picture is the real app at 1280×800, three variants of one knob, swapped in place — press the buttons, or hover a picture and use ← →.

Why 1, and what I most want you to overrule A label is a caption on one field, so it goes where that field's reading starts — the same argument the list row's caption already won. It leaves every control where your hand expects it. The part I am least sure of is section 1's labels on both edges: if a Persian-over-Latin form reads as ragged to you rather than as each label finding its word, that is the case for 0 — which is also exactly what "rule the chrome out of scope" would ship.
